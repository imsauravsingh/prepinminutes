# PrepInMinutes

> **Get interview-ready in minutes.**

PrepInMinutes is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed for software engineers. It transforms a candidate's **resume, target role, job description, preparation progress, and practice performance** into a personalized interview preparation journey.

## 🎯 Product Vision

Help engineers stop preparing randomly and instead follow a **personalized, measurable, continuously improving preparation plan**.

**Core loop:**

`Understand → Plan → Practice → Evaluate → Improve → Revise → Repeat`

## 💡 Problem

Interview preparation is often fragmented across YouTube, courses, question banks, coding platforms, notes, and mock interviews. Candidates struggle to know:

- What should I prepare?
- What is important for my target role?
- Where am I weak?
- Am I actually interview-ready?
- What should I practice next?

## 🚀 Solution

PrepInMinutes brings the complete preparation journey into one platform.

### Core Capabilities

- **Resume Intelligence** — Understand experience, skills and likely interview questions.
- **Job Description Analysis** — Identify required skills and probable interview areas.
- **Personalized Preparation Plan** — Prioritize what to study based on role, experience and timeline.
- **Smart Practice** — Generate relevant technical and role-specific questions.
- **AI Mock Interviews** — Simulate realistic interview sessions.
- **Evaluation** — Score technical knowledge, problem solving, system design, communication and depth.
- **Weakness Detection** — Identify recurring gaps from practice history.
- **Smart Revision** — Bring weak topics back into the preparation cycle.
- **AI Recommendations** — Recommend the next best preparation action.

## 🧠 AI Intelligence

The AI layer uses candidate context, resume, JD, preparation history, practice answers and evaluation results to provide personalized recommendations rather than generic questions.
