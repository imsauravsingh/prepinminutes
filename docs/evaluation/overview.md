# Evaluation Hub — Overview

The **Evaluation Hub** (`/evaluation`) provides candidates with transparent, data-driven feedback on their overall interview readiness, domain strengths, historical performance trends, and focus areas.

---

## 1. Core Purpose

1. **Answer "Am I Ready?"**: Replace subjective guesswork with a unified, mathematical **Readiness Percentage (0–100%)** calibrated against real hiring bars for the candidate's target role and company tier.
2. **Track Trajectory**: Visualize readiness growth over time using weekly line/area progress graphs.
3. **Surface Blind Spots**: Categorize evaluation marks by domain (System Design, Algorithms, Behavioral, Code Quality) to pinpoint areas needing immediate reinforcement.
4. **Actionable Insights**: Provide clear written highlights of top strengths and high-priority focus topics for the upcoming week.

---

## 2. Interaction With Other Modules

- **Practice & Mock Interviews**: Every submitted practice drill and mock interview produces an evaluation event that streams into the Evaluation Hub.
- **Preparation Plan**: Evaluation scores automatically adjust topic prioritization and phase completion in the preparation plan.
- **Revision Queue**: Topics scoring $< 60\%$ in evaluations are automatically queued into the Revision module.
